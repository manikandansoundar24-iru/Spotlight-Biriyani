# Business playbook — how to actually earn from this

*Indicative Indian market pricing (2026). Adjust to your city and his turnover.*

## 1. Know his numbers before you quote
Ask: plates/day, average bill, Swiggy+Zomato share, current billing method
(notebook / Petpooja / paper). A 3-branch biriyani shop doing ~250 orders/day at
₹200 average = ~₹15 lakh/month revenue. Software at ₹2,000–5,000/month is under 0.3%
of revenue — frame it that way, never as "an app".

## 2. Pricing options

**A. One-time build + AMC (easiest first sale)**
- Website + owner app, 3 branches, setup + training: **₹35,000 – ₹60,000**
- Annual maintenance (updates, backups, phone support): **18% of project/yr**

**B. Monthly subscription (better long-term)**
| Plan | Price / month | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | ₹999 / branch | Billing, stock, reports, website |
| Growth | ₹1,999 / branch | + cloud sync, multi-user logins, WhatsApp daily summary |
| Pro | ₹3,499 / branch | + recipe-wise stock deduction, GST reports, priority support |

3 branches on Growth = ₹5,997/month = ₹71,964/year from one client.
Add a one-time onboarding fee of ₹9,999 (setup, cost sheet, staff training).

**C. Hybrid (recommended)** — ₹25,000 setup + ₹1,499/branch/month. Low entry, recurring income.

## 3. Sales script (use his own pain)
> "You told me you can't say how many chicken biriyani vs mutton biriyani you sold
> yesterday, and you don't know if Zomato actually leaves you profit. Open this —
> here is yesterday: 62 chicken, 18 mutton, 24 kushka. Zomato collected ₹46,580,
> commission took ₹11,179, food cost ₹22,000 — you kept ₹15,293, 32.8% margin.
> Counter margin is 47.5%. Same data every day, one screen, and it tells your cook
> how many plates to prepare tomorrow."

Then let him tap a bill himself. The demo closes the sale, not the slides.

## 4. Free trial that converts
14 days on his real data at ONE branch. Enter his true plate costs during setup —
the "true profit" number is the moment he becomes a customer. Do not give a trial
without entering real costs; the app looks like a toy without them.

## 5. Scaling to more clients
- Every biriyani shop, tiffin centre, cloud kitchen and bakery has the same problem.
- Rebrand time is under an hour (see `docs/CUSTOMISE.md`).
- 20 shops × ₹1,999/month = ₹39,980/month recurring.
- Ask for a video testimonial + Google review from Spot Light and reuse it.
- Referral offer: one free month for every shop he refers.

## 6. Things to promise carefully
- Aggregator order auto-import needs Swiggy/Zomato partner API approval — quote as
  a separate project, never bundle it.
- GST filing is his CA's job; you export data, you don't file returns.
- Data currently lives on the device — sell the cloud upgrade for multi-branch
  visibility rather than over-promising it today.

## 7. Delivery checklist per client
1. Rebrand (name, menu, photos, colours) — `docs/CUSTOMISE.md`
2. Deploy + custom domain — `docs/SETUP.md`
3. Enter real prices, plate costs, commissions, stock — 1 hour with the owner
4. Train counter staff on Billing only (10 minutes; that's all they need)
5. Train the owner on Dashboard + Reports (15 minutes)
6. Run `docs/TESTING.md`, hand over a backup file, set a weekly backup reminder
7. Invoice, collect, and schedule a 30-day review call to upsell the next module
