## 1. Executive Summary: The Restaurant Reality

Every restaurant owner (especially in high-volume categories like Biriyani, Fast Food, South Indian, and Cloud Kitchens) wakes up with 4 major headaches:
1. **Cash Pilferage & Food Leakage:** Staff selling plates off-the-counter and pocketing cash, or giving free plates to friends.
2. **Aggregator Commission Bleed:** Swiggy and Zomato taking 22% to 26% of every order while owners don't know if they made a profit or a loss on an online order.
3. **Expensive Legacy Software:** Companies like Petpooja, Posist, and Pine Labs demanding ₹15,000 to ₹40,000 upfront, forcing owners to buy ₹35,000 touchscreen POS hardware and charging hefty annual renewal fees.
4. **Internet & Device Dependency:** Systems crashing when Wi-Fi drops, leaving customers waiting in line.

**RestoPulse solves every single one of these problems on day one.**
