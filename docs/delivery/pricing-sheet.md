# Spot Light Manager — Pricing Sheet for Restaurant Owners

## Recommended pricing options

### Option 1 — One-time setup + support
Best for owners who want a fixed project cost and a clear implementation package.

Suggested range:
- Setup and onboarding: ₹35,000 – ₹60,000
- Annual support / maintenance: 15% – 20% of project value

### Option 2 — Monthly subscription
Best for recurring revenue, long-term support, and ongoing upgrades.

Suggested range:
- Starter plan: ₹999 / month per branch
  - Basic billing, stock, and reporting
  - Suitable for a single small shop or first-time adoption
- Growth plan: ₹1,999 / month per branch
  - Includes more reporting, more operational visibility, and better support
  - Suitable for growing restaurants and multi-branch operations
- Pro plan: ₹3,499 / month per branch
  - Advanced reporting, deeper business insights, premium support, and future-ready modules
  - Suitable for larger businesses that need stronger control and scalability

### Option 3 — Hybrid model
Best balance for first-time customers who want a lower entry cost but future expansion options.

Suggested range:
- Setup fee: ₹25,000
- Monthly support: ₹1,499 / branch

## What is included in each plan

### Starter plan
- system setup
- menu and price configuration
- basic stock setup
- staff onboarding
- basic reports and dashboard access
- standard support

### Growth plan
- everything in Starter
- enhanced reporting
- branch-level visibility
- better support response
- access to additional operational modules over time

### Pro plan
- everything in Growth
- advanced reporting and analytics
- deeper profitability insights
- premium support
- priority upgrades and future feature rollout

## Optional add-ons
- cloud backup and multi-device access
- branch-wise reporting
- advanced accounting and expense tracking
- payroll and attendance
- WhatsApp reporting
- loyalty and customer management
- custom integrations and API-based workflows

## Suggested sales message
This is not just software. It is a practical business tool that helps the owner control billing, stock, and profit more professionally while reducing daily chaos.

## Suggested client conversation
If the client asks about price, the best approach is:
- explain the value in terms of saving time and improving profit visibility
- compare it to the cost of manual errors, stock loss, and poor reporting
- position it as an operational investment, not just an app cost
- show them that each plan matches a different business stage and support need
